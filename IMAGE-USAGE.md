# Image usage review

The magazine uses a different photograph at every image placement. The original opening, editor detail, motion interlude, loom macro and green chapter each appear once.

## New story-specific images

- Global Fashion Directions: unique chromatic tailoring, constructed fluidity, material memory and ornament images.
- The Body & The Cloth: five independent studies of drape, fold, wrap, pleat and move.
- Craft × Future: an experimental material still life, distinct from the traditional loom story.

## Purposeful continuity

The One Cloth / Three Languages study uses one coordinated triptych of the same scarlet textile in three different silhouettes. Its three tabs show different portions of that image; no look is repeated elsewhere in the magazine.

The portfolio presentation reuses selected editorial imagery to explain art direction and shows screenshots of the actual product. Desktop and mobile versions of the cover and green chapter demonstrate responsive composition. These are deliberate documentation, not duplicated magazine stories.

All new imagery is AI-generated and conceptual, not documentary photography.
